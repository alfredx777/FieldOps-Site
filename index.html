Every job follows the same chain, so nothing gets lost between the people who plan the work and the people who do it.

One app that keeps the office and the field working from the same job.

Built for service businesses whose work happens at the customer’s premises. Office staff plan and track every job, and technicians capture the work on site, with or without signal.

  • Air conditioning

Office

Keep customers, sites and equipment in one place. Create, assign, reschedule and close work orders, with search, filters and an archive.

Technicians

See only your own jobs, with access notes and equipment details. Record the diagnosis, parts used, photos and the customer’s signature.

Offline

No signal? Keep working. Changes and photos are saved on the device and sync when the connection returns.

Reports

Generate a PDF service report with photos and signature. Download it, or email it to the customer in one step.

Alerts

Optional phone alerts when a job is assigned, rescheduled or cancelled.

Everywhere

One app for Windows desktop and phones. Install it from the browser, no app store needed.

End-to-end workflow

  1. Invite your team

    An admin adds people by email and gives each a role. They choose their own password on first sign-in.

  2. Set up the customer

    Add the customer, their sites, gate instructions, and the equipment; including serial numbers and warranty dates.

  3. Create and assign

    Raise a work order against a piece of equipment and give it to a technician.

  4. Work on site

    The technician starts the job, notes the diagnosis, adds parts and photos, and puts it on hold if needed.

  5. Get the signature

    The customer signs on the screen. The technician writes the resolution and completes the job.

  6. Review and close

    The office checks the job, sends the report and closes the work order.

Offline and sync

A job can also go On hold and resume, and the office can cancel any unfinished job. Completing a job requires a resolution.

Three roles, enforced by the server.

Permissions are checked in the API on every request, not just hidden in the screens.

Admin

Owner or manager

  • Everything a dispatcher does
  • Invite, disable and manage users
  • Review activity
  • Permanently delete archived data

Dispatcher

Office staff

  • Manage customers, sites, equipment
  • Create, assign, reassign, cancel and close jobs
  • Download and email reports

Technician

Field staff

  • See only their own jobs
  • Start, hold, resume and complete
  • Add notes, parts, photos, signature

Small, serverless and careful with your data.

No credentials on the device

Apps hold only the user’s sign-in token. Photos and reports travel through short-lived secure links.

Safe retries

If a connection drops mid-save, a retried change is applied once. Conflicting edits are caught, not overwritten.

Light by design

No heavy UI kits and no PDF libraries on the device. Reports are built on the server.
